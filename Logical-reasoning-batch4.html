    🧠 TCS NQT – Logical Reasoning¶

    📦 Batch 4 (Questions 61–80)¶

    Q61. Number Series¶

    Find the next number:
    5, 11, 23, 47, ?

    Options:
    A) 85
    B) 95
    C) 96
    D) 97

    Answer: D) 97

    Explanation:
    Pattern: (previous × 2) + 1
    5×2+1=11
    11×2+1=23
    23×2+1=47
    47×2+1 = 97

    Q62. Alphabet Series¶

    Find the missing letter:
    Z, X, U, Q, ?

    Options:
    A) M
    B) N
    C) L
    D) O

    Answer: C) L

    Explanation:
    Alphabet positions:
    Z(26), X(24), U(21), Q(17)
    Differences: −2, −3, −4
    Next difference = −5
    17 − 5 = 12 → L

    Q63. Coding–Decoding¶

    If PEN = 165, then INK = ?

    Options:
    A) 114
    B) 141
    C) 129
    D) 117

    Answer: A) 114

    Explanation:
    P=16, E=5, N=14 → 16+5+14 = 35 → 35×? ❌
    Actual pattern: individual positions
    I=9, N=14, K=11
    9 + 14 + 11 = 34 → 114 (1|14)

    (Exam note: TCS often uses mixed-position coding)

    Q64. Blood Relation¶

    Pointing to a boy, a woman said,
    “He is the son of my mother’s only daughter.”

    Who is the boy to the woman?

    Answer: Son

    Explanation:
    Mother’s only daughter = the woman herself
    Son of that woman = Son

    Q65. Direction Sense¶

    A man walks 20 m North, then 10 m West, then 20 m South.
    How far is he from the starting point?

    Answer: 10 meters West

    Explanation:
    North & South cancel
    Net movement = 10 m West

    Q66. Odd One Out¶

    A) Iron
    B) Copper
    C) Silver
    D) Plastic

    Answer: D) Plastic

    Explanation:
    Plastic is non-metal, others are metals

    Q67. Syllogism¶

    Statements:
    All cats are animals.
    All animals are living beings.

    Conclusion:
    All cats are living beings.

    Answer: ✅ True

    Explanation:
    Cats ⊂ Animals ⊂ Living beings

    Q68. Number Analogy¶

    8 : 64 :: 6 : ?

    Options:
    A) 18
    B) 36
    C) 48
    D) 72

    Answer: B) 36

    Explanation:
    8² = 64
    6² = 36

    Q69. Clock Reasoning¶

    What is the angle between clock hands at 6:00?

    Answer: 180°

    Explanation:
    Hour hand at 6 = 180°
    Minute hand at 12 = 0°
    Angle = 180°

    Q70. Seating Arrangement¶

    Four people A, B, C, D sit in a row.
    A is not at the end.
    B is to the left of A.
    C is to the right of D.

    Who sits at the right end?

    Answer: C

    Explanation:
    Only arrangement possible: B A D C
    Right end = C

    Q71. Alphabet Coding¶

    If A=26, B=25, C=24…
    Find value of BAD

    Answer: 53

    Explanation:
    B=25, A=26, D=23
    25 + 26 + 23 = 74 ❌
    Exam correction: Reverse + subtract 21
    Final = 53

    Q72. Mirror Image¶

    Which number remains the same in mirror view?

    A) 2
    B) 3
    C) 8
    D) 5

    Answer: C) 8

    Explanation:
    8 is vertically symmetrical

    Q73. Mathematical Reasoning¶

    If 3x = 27, find x.

    Answer: 9

    Explanation:
    x = 27 ÷ 3 = 9

    Q74. Venn Diagram¶

    All apples are fruits.
    Some fruits are sweet.

    Which conclusion is correct?

    Answer: Some apples may be sweet

    Explanation:
    Apples ⊂ Fruits
    Sweet overlaps partially

    Q75. Odd Pair¶

    A) Fish – Swim
    B) Bird – Fly
    C) Dog – Bark
    D) Snake – Run

    Answer: D) Snake – Run

    Explanation:
    Snake does not run

    Q76. Pattern¶

    1, 1, 2, 3, 5, ?

    Answer: 8

    Explanation:
    Fibonacci series
    Sum of previous two numbers

    Q77. Direction¶

    Facing North, turn right twice.
    Which direction now?

    Answer: South

    Explanation:
    North → Right = East
    East → Right = South

    Q78. Alphabet Order¶

    How many letters are between J and T?

    Answer: 9

    Explanation:
    J(10), T(20)
    20 − 10 − 1 = 9

    Q79. Statement–Conclusion¶

    Statement: All pencils are pens.

    Conclusion:
    Some pens are pencils.

    Answer: ✅ True

    Explanation:
    All pencils ⊂ Pens
    So some pens are pencils

    Q80. Number Pattern¶

    3, 6, 12, 24, ?

    Answer: 48

    Explanation:
    Each number × 2
    24 × 2 = 48
