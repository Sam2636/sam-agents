    🧠 TCS NQT – Logical Reasoning¶

    📦 Batch 3 (Questions 41–60)¶

    Q41. Number Series¶

    Find the next number:
    2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

    Options:
    A) 36
    B) 40
    C) 42
    D) 44

    Answer: C) 42

    Explanation:
    Pattern = n × (n+1)
    1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30
    Next = 6×7 = 42

    Q42. Alphabet Series¶

    Find the missing letter:
    A, D, H, M, ?

    Options:
    A) Q
    B) R
    C) S
    D) T

    Answer: B) R

    Explanation:
    Alphabet positions:
    A(1), D(4), H(8), M(13)
    Differences: +3, +4, +5
    Next difference = +6
    13 + 6 = 19 → R

    Q43. Coding–Decoding¶

    If DOG = 415, then CAT = ?

    Options:
    A) 312
    B) 314
    C) 213
    D) 214

    Answer: B) 314

    Explanation:
    Alphabet positions:
    D=4, O=15, G=7 → DOG = 415
    C=3, A=1, T=20 → CAT = 314

    Q44. Blood Relation¶

    Pointing to a woman, Ravi said,
    “She is the sister of my father’s only son.”

    Who is the woman?

    Answer: Ravi’s Aunt

    Explanation:
    Father’s only son = Ravi
    Sister of Ravi = Father’s daughter → Aunt

    Q45. Direction Sense¶

    A man walks 10 m North, then 10 m East, then 10 m South.
    How far is he from the starting point?

    Answer: 10 meters East

    Explanation:
    North and South cancel out
    Net movement = 10 m East

    Q46. Odd One Out¶

    Choose the odd one:
    A) Square
    B) Rectangle
    C) Circle
    D) Triangle

    Answer: C) Circle

    Explanation:
    Circle has no sides, others are polygons

    Q47. Syllogism¶

    Statements:
    All pens are books.
    Some books are papers.

    Conclusion:
    Some pens are papers.

    Answer: ❌ Cannot be determined

    Explanation:
    No direct link between pens and papers

    Q48. Number Analogy¶

    3 : 27 :: 4 : ?

    Options:
    A) 64
    B) 81
    C) 16
    D) 256

    Answer: A) 64

    Explanation:
    3³ = 27
    4³ = 64

    Q49. Clock Reasoning¶

    What is the angle between the hour and minute hand at 3:00?

    Answer: 90°

    Explanation:
    Hour hand at 3 → 90°
    Minute hand at 12 → 0°
    Difference = 90°

    Q50. Seating Arrangement¶

    Five people A, B, C, D, E sit in a row.
    B is between A and C.
    D is at the left end.
    E is at the right end.

    Who sits in the middle?

    Answer: B

    Explanation:
    Arrangement: D A B C E
    Middle = B

    Q51. Alphabet Coding¶

    If A=1, B=2, … Z=26
    Find value of BAT

    Answer: 23

    Explanation:
    B=2, A=1, T=20
    2 + 1 + 20 = 23

    Q52. Mirror Image¶

    Which letter looks the same in a mirror (vertical)?

    Options:
    A) B
    B) C
    C) D
    D) E

    Answer: B) C

    Explanation:
    C is vertically symmetrical

    Q53. Number Puzzle¶

    If 5 + 3 = 28
    6 + 4 = 40
    Then 7 + 5 = ?

    Answer: 54

    Explanation:
    Formula: (a + b) × 4
    (7 + 5) × 4 = 54

    Q54. Direction¶

    Facing East, turn right, then left, then right.
    Which direction are you facing?

    Answer: South

    Explanation:
    East → Right = South
    South → Left = East
    East → Right = South

    Q55. Series Completion¶

    1, 4, 9, 16, ?

    Answer: 25

    Explanation:
    Perfect squares:
    1², 2², 3², 4² → 5² = 25

    Q56. Analogy¶

    Eye : See :: Ear : ?

    Answer: Hear

    Explanation:
    Eye is used to see
    Ear is used to hear

    Q57. Mathematical Reasoning¶

    If x + 5 = 12, what is x?

    Answer: 7

    Explanation:
    x = 12 − 5 = 7

    Q58. Venn Diagram¶

    All roses are flowers.
    Some flowers are red.

    Which is correct?

    Answer: Some roses may be red

    Explanation:
    Roses ⊂ Flowers
    Some flowers are red → overlap possible

    Q59. Odd Pair¶

    Which pair is odd?

    A) Pen – Write
    B) Knife – Cut
    C) Chair – Sit
    D) Clock – Eat

    Answer: D) Clock – Eat

    Explanation:
    Clock is not used for eating

    Q60. Pattern¶

    2, 4, 8, 16, ?

    Answer: 32

    Explanation:
    Each number × 2
    16 × 2 = 32
