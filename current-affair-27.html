    📅 SBI/IBPS Daily Current Affairs¶

    Date: 27 June 2026

    🏦 Banking & Economy¶

    1. RBI Strengthens Customer Protection Against Digital Payment Fraud¶

    📰 What Happened?¶

    The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has introduced a stronger customer protection framework against unauthorized digital payment fraud.

    The framework introduces a Shadow Reversal mechanism. Under this mechanism, eligible customers may receive a temporary credit while the bank investigates the disputed transaction.

    The revised framework also extends protection to sole proprietorships, ensuring that more customers are covered.

    📖 Background¶

    India has witnessed explosive growth in digital payments through:

    • UPI
    • IMPS
    • NEFT
    • RTGS
    • Debit Cards
    • Credit Cards

    With the increase in digital transactions, cyber fraud has also increased.

    RBI periodically updates customer protection guidelines to improve confidence in digital banking.

    ⭐ Why is this important?¶

    • Improves trust in digital banking.
    • Encourages cashless transactions.
    • Protects customers against financial loss.
    • Strengthens cybersecurity in banking.

    📌 Key Facts¶

    Particular Details
    Regulator RBI
    Topic Digital Banking
    Keyword Shadow Reversal
    Objective Customer Protection
    Covers Unauthorized Digital Payment Fraud

    🧠 Static GK¶

    RBI Headquarters: Mumbai

    Governor: Sanjay Malhotra

    Established: 1 April 1935

    Nationalized: 1 January 1949

    🎯 Expected SBI/IBPS Questions¶

    1. Which organization introduced Shadow Reversal?
    2. What is the purpose of Shadow Reversal?
    3. Which customers are protected under the new framework?
    4. Which regulator issues digital banking guidelines?

    2. Bank Holiday on 27 June 2026¶

    📰 What Happened?¶

    Banks remained closed in several states due to the Muharram holiday.

    However, digital banking services continued without interruption.

    Services available:

    • UPI
    • IMPS
    • NEFT
    • RTGS
    • Internet Banking
    • Mobile Banking

    📖 Background¶

    Bank holidays are notified by RBI under the Negotiable Instruments Act.

    Some holidays are state-specific.

    ⭐ Why is it important?¶

    Many SBI and IBPS questions ask whether digital services continue during bank holidays.

    📌 Key Facts¶

    • Branch banking depends on the holiday calendar.
    • UPI works 24×7.
    • Internet Banking works 24×7.

    🎯 Expected Questions¶

    • Who issues the bank holiday calendar?
    • Which services remain operational during holidays?

    📰 What Happened?¶

    The World Bank projected India's GDP growth for FY2026–27 at 6.6%.

    The report states that India continues to remain one of the fastest-growing major economies.

    📖 Background¶

    The World Bank regularly publishes economic outlook reports for member countries.

    GDP forecasts help governments and investors understand future economic growth.

    GDP projections are frequently asked in banking examinations.

    4. India–UK Trade Discussions¶

    📰 What Happened?¶

    Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal visited the United Kingdom to discuss the implementation of the India–UK Trade Agreement.

    The discussions focused on:

    • Tariff reduction
    • Trade facilitation
    • Investment
    • Business cooperation

    📖 Background¶

    A Free Trade Agreement (FTA) reduces trade barriers between countries.

    It promotes:

    • Exports
    • Imports
    • Investment
    • Employment

    ⭐ Why is it important?¶

    FTAs are frequently asked in Banking and SSC examinations.

    📌 Key Facts¶

    Country: United Kingdom

    Topic: Trade Agreement

    Shadow Reversal is related to:

    A. GST

    B. Digital Payment Fraud

    C. Insurance

    D. Income Tax

    Answer: B

    Explanation: Shadow Reversal provides temporary credit to eligible customers during investigation of digital payment fraud.
