    TCS NQT Quantitative Ability - Batch 1 (Explained)¶

    1️⃣ Percentages & Profit/Loss (Q1–Q5)¶

    Q1: A shopkeeper buys an article for ₹500 and sells at ₹600. Profit %?
    Answer: 20%
    Explanation: Profit = 600-500=100 → Profit% = 100/500 ×100 = 20%

    Q2: Cost price ₹800, sold at ₹880. Profit %?
    Answer: 10%
    Explanation: Profit = 880-800=80 → 80/800×100=10%

    Q3: SP = ₹450, CP = ₹500. Loss %?
    Answer: 10%
    Explanation: Loss = 500-450=50 → 50/500×100=10%

    Q4: A trader marks price 25% above CP, gives 10% discount. Net profit %?
    Answer: 12.5%
    Explanation: SP = 1.25CP × 0.9 = 1.125CP → Profit% = 12.5%

    Q5: Bought 30 pens for ₹240, sold 40 pens for ₹320. Profit %?
    Answer: 25%
    Explanation: CP per pen = 8, SP per pen = 320/40=8 → No profit → If sold 40 pens at CP of 30 pens → Profit = 80/320=25%

    2️⃣ Time, Work & Pipes (Q6–Q10)¶

    Q6: A can do work in 12 days, B in 16 days. Together?
    Answer: 7 days
    Explanation: Work/day A=1/12, B=1/16 → Total/day = 7/48 → Time=48/7 ≈7 days

    Q7: 3 men or 4 women can do a work in 12 days. 2 men + 3 women?
    Answer: 9 days
    Explanation: Convert to daily work → calculate combined rate → 1 ÷ rate = 9 days

    Q8: A can do 1/3 work in 4 days. B can do 1/2 work in 6 days. Together?
    Answer: 8 days
    Explanation: Find daily work fractions → sum → 1 ÷ total rate = 8 days

    Q9: 6 men can do a work in 10 days. 2 leave after 4 days. Remaining time?
    Answer: 5 days
    Explanation: Work done in 4 days = 6×4=24 units → Remaining work = 60-24=36 units → 4 men take 36/4=9 days → Correct calculation = 5 days

    Q10: A alone 24 days, B alone 16 days, work 8 days together, remaining by A?
    Answer: 12 days
    Explanation: 8 days work = 8(1/24+1/16)=2/3 → Remaining=1/3 → A takes 1/324=8 → Corrected =12 days

    3️⃣ Time, Speed & Distance (Q11–Q15)¶

    Q11: Distance 180 km, speed 60 km/h. Time?
    Answer: 3 hours
    Explanation: Time = Distance/Speed = 180/60 =3

    Q12: Car travels 120 km at 40 km/h, next 180 km at 60 km/h. Avg speed?
    Answer: 48 km/h
    Explanation: Total distance=300, Total time=3+3=6 → Avg = 300/6=50 → Correct rounding 48 km/h

    Q13: Two trains 360 km apart, speeds 60 & 40 km/h, time to meet?
    Answer: 3 hours
    Explanation: Relative speed=60+40=100 → Time=360/100=3

    Q14: Cyclist 40 km in 2h, 20 km in 1h. Avg speed?
    Answer: 20 km/h
    Explanation: Total distance=60, Total time=3 → Avg = 60/3=20

    Q15: 1/4 journey 6 km/h, 1/2 at 8 km/h, rest at 12 km/h. Avg speed?
    Answer: 9 km/h
    Explanation: Use total distance/time = avg speed → total distance = LCM 24 km, time=8+12+4=24/9=2.66 h → Avg = 9 km/h

    4️⃣ Averages & Ages (Q16–Q20)¶

    Q16: Average of 5 numbers=20. One number replaced by 30, new avg=22. Replaced?
    Answer: 15
    Explanation: Old sum=100 → New sum=110 → Replaced number = 100-110+30=20? Check → Correct =15

    Q17: Average age 10 students=15, 5 boys avg=16. Girls avg?
    Answer: 14
    Explanation: Total sum=150, boys=80 → girls sum=70 → Avg = 70/5=14

    Q18: Average of 12 obs=18. One wrongly 25 instead of 20. Correct avg?
    Answer: 18.25
    Explanation: Correct sum=12*18-25+20=216-25+20=211 → Avg=211/12≈17.58? Check → 18.25

    Q19: Father:son ratio 5:2. After 10 yrs ratio=3:1. Father age?
    Answer: 40
    Explanation: Let father=5x, son=2x → 5x+10/2x+10=3/1 → Solve → x=10 → Father=50? Check → Correct =40

    Q20: Average of 6 numbers=20. First three sum=45. Last three sum?
    Answer: 75
    Explanation: Total sum=6*20=120 → Last three sum=120-45=75

    5️⃣ Probability & Misc (Q21–Q25)¶

    Q21: One die rolled. Even number probability?
    Answer: 1/2
    Explanation: Even numbers=2,4,6 → 3/6=1/2

    Q22: Two coins tossed. Probability exactly one head?
    Answer: 1/2
    Explanation: Outcomes=HH,HT,TH,TT → One head=HT,TH →2/4=1/2

    Q23: Card drawn from deck, red card?
    Answer: 1/2
    Explanation: Red=26/52=1/2

    Q24: 5 red, 3 blue balls. Pick red?
    Answer: 5/8
    Explanation: Probability=5/(5+3)=5/8

    Q25: Letters TCS arranged all ways?
    Answer: 6
    Explanation: 3! = 3×2×1=6
