    🧠 Logical Reasoning — Batch 1 (20 / 100)¶

    TCS NQT Exam Style — With Step-by-Step Explanations

    🔢 1. Number & Alphabet Series¶

    Q1¶

    2, 6, 12, 20, ?

    Explanation: - 1 × 2 = 2 - 2 × 3 = 6 - 3 × 4 = 12 - 4 × 5 = 20

    Next term: - 5 × 6 = 30

    Answer: 30

    Q2¶

    3, 9, 27, ?, 243

    Explanation: - Each term is multiplied by 3

    3 × 3 = 9
    9 × 3 = 27
    27 × 3 = 81
    81 × 3 = 243

    Answer: 81

    Q3¶

    A, C, F, J, O, ?

    Explanation: - Alphabet positions: - A = 1 - C = 3 - F = 6 - J = 10 - O = 15 - Differences: - +2, +3, +4, +5

    Next difference = +6
    15 + 6 = 21 → U

    Answer: U

    Q4¶

    Find the wrong term:
    2, 4, 8, 14, 32

    Explanation: - Expected pattern: powers of 2
    - 2¹ = 2 - 2² = 4 - 2³ = 8 - 2⁴ = 16 - 2⁵ = 32 - Given value 14 breaks the pattern

    Answer: 14

    Q5¶

    1, 4, 9, 16, ?

    Explanation: - Squares of natural numbers: - 1², 2², 3², 4²

    Next = 5² = 25

    Answer: 25

    🔐 2. Coding–Decoding¶

    Q6¶

    CAT → DBU
    Find DOG → ?

    Explanation: - Each letter is shifted one position forward - C → D - A → B - T → U

    Apply same rule: - D → E - O → P - G → H

    Answer: EPH

    Q7¶

    PEN → QFO
    Find INK → ?

    Explanation: - Each letter moves one step forward - I → J - N → O - K → L

    Answer: JOL

    Q8¶

    123 → ABC
    Find 456 → ?

    Explanation: - 1 = A, 2 = B, 3 = C - 4 = D, 5 = E, 6 = F

    Answer: DEF

    Q9¶

    MANGO → 13114715
    Find APPLE → ?

    Explanation: - Letter positions: - A = 1 - P = 16 - P = 16 - L = 12 - E = 5

    Concatenating values → 11616125

    Answer: 11616125

    👨‍👩‍👦 3. Blood Relations¶

    Q10¶

    "He is the son of my grandfather’s only son."

    Explanation: - Grandfather’s only son = Father - Son of father = Brother

    Answer: Brother

    Q11¶

    A is B’s sister.
    B is C’s mother.

    Explanation: - A is sister of C’s mother

    Answer: Aunt

    Q12¶

    "She is my mother’s daughter but not my sister."

    Explanation: - Mother’s daughter = self or sister - Not sister → self

    Answer: Self

    Q13¶

    X is father of Y.
    Y is father of Z.

    Explanation: - X → Y → Z

    Answer: Grandfather

    🧭 4. Direction Sense¶

    Q14¶

    A man walks 10 m north, then 5 m east, then 10 m south.
    How far is he from the starting point?

    Explanation: - North and south cancel each other - Remaining movement = 5 m east

    Answer: 5 m

    Q15¶

    Facing east, Ravi turns left, then right, then right.
    Which direction is he facing now?

    Explanation: - East → Left → North - North → Right → East - East → Right → South

    Answer: South

    Q16¶

    A person walks 20 m west, 10 m south, then 20 m east.
    Find the distance from the starting point.

    Explanation: - West and east cancel each other - Remaining movement = 10 m south

    Answer: 10 m

    🧩 5. Logical Statements¶

    Q17¶

    All pens are books.
    All books are bags.
    Conclusion: All pens are bags.

    Explanation: - Pens ⊂ Books ⊂ Bags

    Answer: True

    Q18¶

    Some apples are mangoes.
    Some mangoes are oranges.

    Explanation: - No direct link between apples and oranges

    Answer: Cannot be determined

    Q19¶

    If today is Monday, what day will it be after 15 days?

    Explanation: - 15 mod 7 = 1 - Monday + 1 = Tuesday

    Answer: Tuesday

    Q20¶

    Chair, Table, Bed, Car

    Explanation: - Chair, Table, Bed → Furniture - Car → Vehicle

    Answer: Car
