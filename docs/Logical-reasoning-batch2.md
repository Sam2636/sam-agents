## 🔢 1. Number Series

### Q21
**5, 11, 23, 47, ?**

**Explanation:**
- 5 × 2 + 1 = 11
- 11 × 2 + 1 = 23
- 23 × 2 + 1 = 47

Next:
- 47 × 2 + 1 = **95**

**Answer:** 95

---

### Q22
**1, 1, 2, 6, 24, ?**

**Explanation:**
- Factorial pattern:
  - 1! = 1
  - 2! = 2
  - 3! = 6
  - 4! = 24

Next = 5! = **120**

**Answer:** 120

---

### Q23
**2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?**

**Explanation:**
- Pattern: n² + 1
  - 1²+1 = 2
  - 2²+1 = 5
  - 3²+1 = 10
  - 4²+1 = 17
  - 5²+1 = 26

Next = 6² + 1 = **37**

**Answer:** 37

---

### Q24
**100, 81, 64, 49, ?**

**Explanation:**
- Squares in descending order:
  - 10², 9², 8², 7²

Next = 6² = **36**

**Answer:** 36

### Q25
**7, 14, 28, 56, ?**

## 🔐 2. Coding–Decoding

### Q26
If **FLOWER = GMPXFS**, then **GARDEN = ?**

**Explanation:**
- Each letter is shifted +1

G → H  
A → B  
R → S  
D → E  
E → F  
N → O  

**Answer:** HBSEFO

---

### Q27
If **A = 1, Z = 26**, find the value of **DOG**

**Explanation:**
- D = 4
- O = 15
- G = 7

Sum = 4 + 15 + 7 = **26**

**Answer:** 26

### Q28
If **BAT = 21**, **CAT = 24**, then **DOG = ?**

**Explanation:**
- B(2) + A(1) + T(20) = 23 ❌  
  (Given 21 → pattern is sum − 2)

Check CAT:
- C(3)+A(1)+T(20)=24 ✔

Apply to DOG:
- D(4)+O(15)+G(7)=26

**Answer:** 26

## 🧩 3. Logical Puzzles

### Q30
Some pens are pencils.  
All pencils are erasers.  
Conclusion: Some pens are erasers.

**Explanation:**
- Pens ∩ Pencils ≠ ∅
- Pencils ⊂ Erasers

**Answer:** True

## 🧭 5. Direction Sense

### Q36
A person walks 15 m north, then 15 m west, then 15 m south.  
Distance from starting point?

**Explanation:**
- North and south cancel
- Remaining = 15 m west

**Answer:** 15 m

---

### Q37
Facing south, turn right, then left, then left.  
Final direction?

**Explanation:**
- South → Right → West
- West → Left → South
- South → Left → East

**Answer:** East

## 🧠 6. Clocks & Calendars

### Q38
How many times do clock hands overlap in 24 hours?

**Explanation:**
- 11 times in 12 hours
- 22 times in 24 hours

**Answer:** 22

---

### Q39
If today is Friday, what day will it be after 20 days?

**Explanation:**
- 20 mod 7 = 6
- Friday + 6 = Thursday

**Answer:** Thursday
