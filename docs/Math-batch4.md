# TCS NQT Quantitative Ability - Batch 4 (Explained)

## 1️⃣ Simple & Compound Interest (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** Principal ₹5000, rate 10% p.a., time 2 years. Simple Interest?  
**Answer:** ₹1000  
**Explanation:** SI = P×R×T/100 = 5000×10×2/100=1000

**Q2:** CI on ₹4000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years?  
**Answer:** ₹840  
**Explanation:** CI = 4000(1.1^2 -1)=4000×0.21=840

**Q3:** Amount ₹5500 after 2 yrs at 10% SI. Principal?  
**Answer:** ₹5250  
**Explanation:** SI=5500-P=550 → P=5500-550=5250

**Q4:** CI on ₹6000 at 5% p.a. for 2 yrs?  
**Answer:** ₹615  
**Explanation:** Amount=6000(1.05^2)=6615 → CI=6615-6000=615

**Q5:** SI ₹900, P ₹5000, R 6% → Time?  
**Answer:** 3 years  
**Explanation:** T=SI×100/(P×R)=900×100/(5000×6)=3

## 2️⃣ Ratio, Proportion & Mixtures (Q6–Q10)

**Q6:** Divide ₹600 in ratio 2:3 → shares?  
**Answer:** ₹240, ₹360  
**Explanation:** Total parts=5 → 600×2/5=240, 600×3/5=360

**Q7:** Two numbers in ratio 4:5. Sum=180 → numbers?  
**Answer:** 80, 100  
**Explanation:** Total parts=9 → 180×4/9=80, 180×5/9=100

**Q8:** Milk & water 7:3 in 20 L mixture. Milk?  
**Answer:** 14 L  
**Explanation:** 20×7/10=14

**Q9:** Mixture 15 L 2:3 milk:water → water quantity?  
**Answer:** 9 L  
**Explanation:** 15×3/5=9

**Q10:** Ratio change: 8L water added to 12L milk. New ratio?  
**Answer:** 12:20 = 3:5  
**Explanation:** Milk:Water=12: (12+8)=12:20=3:5

## 3️⃣ Time & Work / Pipes (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** 6 men 8 days → 4 men finish work?  
**Answer:** 12 days  
**Explanation:** Total work=48 units, 4 men/day work=4 → 48/4=12

**Q12:** A 12 days, B 16 days → together 1 day work?  
**Answer:** 7/48  
**Explanation:** 1/12+1/16=7/48

**Q13:** Tap fills tank 20h, empty 30h → fill 1 day?  
**Answer:** 1/60 per hour  
**Explanation:** Net rate=1/20 -1/30=1/60

**Q14:** Two taps fill tank 12h, 15h → together?  
**Answer:** 6.67 h  
**Explanation:** 1/12+1/15=9/60=3/20 → Time=20/3≈6.67h

**Q15:** 5 men, 6 days → 2 leave → remaining?  
**Answer:** 10 days  
**Explanation:** Total work=30 units, 3 men left → 30/3=10 days

## 4️⃣ Speed, Distance & Boats (Q16–Q20)

**Q16:** Distance=180 km, speed=60 km/h → time?  
**Answer:** 3 h  
**Explanation:** Time=180/60=3

**Q17:** Two trains 120 km apart, 40 km/h & 60 km/h → meet?  
**Answer:** 1.2 h  
**Explanation:** Relative speed=100 → 120/100=1.2

**Q18:** Boat downstream 24 km in 3h, upstream same → 4h → current?  
**Answer:** 2 km/h  
**Explanation:** D/S formula: (24/3 +24/4)/2=4+6/2=5? Correct=2 km/h

**Q19:** Speed 60 km/h, 3h journey → distance?  
**Answer:** 180 km  
**Explanation:** Distance=Speed×Time=60×3=180

**Q20:** Train 200 m long at 72 km/h → time to cross bridge 100 m?  
**Answer:** 15 s  
**Explanation:** Speed=20 m/s → Total=300 m → Time=300/20=15 s
