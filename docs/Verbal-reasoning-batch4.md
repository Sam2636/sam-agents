## 1️⃣ Synonyms & Vocabulary (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** Synonym for **“Eradicate”**  
a) Remove  
b) Create  
c) Ignore  
d) Support  
**Answer:** a) Remove  

**Q2:** Synonym for **“Prudent”**  
a) Careless  
b) Wise  
c) Rash  
d) Foolish  
**Answer:** b) Wise  

**Q3:** Synonym of **“Inevitable”**  
a) Avoidable  
b) Certain  
c) Unlikely  
d) Doubtful  
**Answer:** b) Certain  

**Q4:** Synonym of **“Vindicate”**  
a) Accuse  
b) Justify  
c) Punish  
d) Blame  
**Answer:** b) Justify  

**Q5:** Synonym of **“Perplexed”**  
a) Confused  
b) Clear  
c) Certain  
d) Smart  
**Answer:** a) Confused

## 3️⃣ Para Jumbles (Q11–Q14)

**Q11:** Arrange:  
A. The sun set behind the hills.  
B. The sky turned orange and red.  
C. People returned to their homes.  
D. Children played for a while before sunset.  
**Answer:** D → A → B → C  

**Q12:** Arrange:  
A. The teacher entered the class.  
B. Students stopped talking.  
C. She started the lesson.  
D. The bell rang.  
**Answer:** D → A → B → C  

**Q13:** Arrange:  
A. The scientist conducted an experiment.  
B. She wrote down the observations.  
C. She prepared the lab carefully.  
D. She analyzed the results.  
**Answer:** C → A → B → D  

**Q14:** Arrange:  
A. The storm caused heavy rainfall.  
B. Roads were flooded.  
C. Authorities issued warnings.  
D. People stayed indoors.  
**Answer:** C → A → B → D

## 4️⃣ Reading Comprehension (Q15–Q20)

**Passage 1:**  
*"Renewable energy sources like solar and wind are becoming more popular. They reduce dependence on fossil fuels and help combat climate change."*  

**Q15:** Primary benefit of renewable energy:  
**Answer:** Reduces dependence on fossil fuels  

**Q16:** What problem does it address?  
**Answer:** Climate change  

**Q17:** Which energy sources are mentioned?  
**Answer:** Solar and wind  

**Passage 2:**  
*"E-learning platforms have transformed education. Students can access courses online, learn at their own pace, and get certificates upon completion."*  

**Q18:** Key advantage of e-learning:  
**Answer:** Learn at own pace  

**Q19:** Benefit of completing courses:  
**Answer:** Obtain certificates  

**Q20:** Implied effect:  
**Answer:** Education is more accessible

## 5️⃣ Critical Reasoning & Sentence Completion (Q21–Q23)

**Q21:** Read: *"If all birds can fly and penguins are birds, can penguins fly?"*  
**Answer:** Not necessarily  

**Q22:** Complete the sentence:  
*"Had I been more careful, I ______ the mistake."*  
a) would avoid  
b) would have avoided  
c) avoid  
d) avoided  
**Answer:** b) would have avoided  

**Q23:** Read: *"All engineers are intelligent. Some intelligent people are teachers. Can we conclude engineers are teachers?"*  
**Answer:** No
