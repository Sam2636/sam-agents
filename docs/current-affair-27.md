# 1. RBI Strengthens Customer Protection Against Digital Payment Fraud

## 📰 What Happened?

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has introduced a stronger customer protection framework against unauthorized digital payment fraud.

The framework introduces a **Shadow Reversal** mechanism. Under this mechanism, eligible customers may receive a temporary credit while the bank investigates the disputed transaction.

The revised framework also extends protection to sole proprietorships, ensuring that more customers are covered.

---

## 📖 Background

India has witnessed explosive growth in digital payments through:

- UPI
- IMPS
- NEFT
- RTGS
- Debit Cards
- Credit Cards

With the increase in digital transactions, cyber fraud has also increased.

RBI periodically updates customer protection guidelines to improve confidence in digital banking.

---

## ⭐ Why is this important?

- Improves trust in digital banking.
- Encourages cashless transactions.
- Protects customers against financial loss.
- Strengthens cybersecurity in banking.

---
