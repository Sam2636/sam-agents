## 3️⃣ Sentence Correction (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** Correct sentence: *"He go to school every day."*  
**Answer:** He **goes** to school every day.  

**Q12:** Correct the sentence: *"She have finished her homework."*  
**Answer:** She **has** finished her homework.  

**Q13:** Correct: *"I did not saw him yesterday."*  
**Answer:** I did not **see** him yesterday.  

**Q14:** Correct: *"Neither of them are interested."*  
**Answer:** Neither of them **is** interested.  

**Q15:** Correct: *"Everyone have received the notification."*  
**Answer:** Everyone **has** received the notification.

## 4️⃣ Para Jumbles (Q16–Q18)

**Q16:** Arrange:  
A. The baby started crying loudly.  
B. The mother picked him up.  
C. He was hungry.  
D. She fed him milk.  
**Answer:** C → A → B → D  

**Q17:** Arrange:  
A. The team gathered for the meeting.  
B. The manager outlined the project plan.  
C. Everyone took notes carefully.  
D. The meeting ended after an hour.  
**Answer:** A → B → C → D  

**Q18:** Arrange:  
A. The storm approached the town.  
B. People secured their homes.  
C. Winds began to blow violently.  
D. Authorities warned residents in advance.  
**Answer:** D → A → C → B

## 5️⃣ Reading Comprehension (Q19–Q23)

**Passage 1:**  
*"Artificial Intelligence is rapidly evolving. It is being used in healthcare, education, and transportation to improve efficiency and decision-making."*  

**Q19:** AI is being used to improve:  
a) Efficiency and decision-making  
b) Only healthcare  
c) Only education  
d) Entertainment  
**Answer:** a) Efficiency and decision-making  

**Q20:** Which sector is NOT mentioned?  
a) Healthcare  
b) Transportation  
c) Education  
d) Agriculture  
**Answer:** d) Agriculture  

**Passage 2:**  
*"Reading regularly enhances vocabulary, comprehension, and critical thinking skills."*  

**Q21:** Which skill is enhanced by reading?  
**Answer:** Vocabulary, comprehension, and critical thinking  

**Q22:** What is a direct benefit of regular reading?  
**Answer:** Improves comprehension and critical thinking  

**Q23:** Which of the following is implied by the passage?  
a) Reading is useless  
b) Reading helps mental growth  
c) Reading only improves vocabulary  
d) Reading decreases intelligence  
**Answer:** b) Reading helps mental growth
