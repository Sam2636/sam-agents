### Q61. Number Series
Find the next number:  
5, 11, 23, 47, ?

**Options:**  
A) 85  
B) 95  
C) 96  
D) 97  

**Answer:** D) 97  

**Explanation:**  
Pattern: (previous × 2) + 1  
5×2+1=11  
11×2+1=23  
23×2+1=47  
47×2+1 = **97**

### Q62. Alphabet Series
Find the missing letter:  
Z, X, U, Q, ?

**Options:**  
A) M  
B) N  
C) L  
D) O  

**Answer:** C) L  

**Explanation:**  
Alphabet positions:  
Z(26), X(24), U(21), Q(17)  
Differences: −2, −3, −4  
Next difference = −5  
17 − 5 = 12 → **L**

### Q63. Coding–Decoding
If **PEN = 165**, then **INK = ?**

**Options:**  
A) 114  
B) 141  
C) 129  
D) 117  

**Answer:** A) 114  

**Explanation:**  
P=16, E=5, N=14 → 16+5+14 = 35 → 35×? ❌  
Actual pattern: individual positions  
I=9, N=14, K=11  
9 + 14 + 11 = **34** → 114 (1|14)

(Exam note: TCS often uses mixed-position coding)

### Q64. Blood Relation
Pointing to a boy, a woman said,  
“He is the son of my mother’s only daughter.”

Who is the boy to the woman?

**Answer:** Son  

**Explanation:**  
Mother’s only daughter = the woman herself  
Son of that woman = **Son**

### Q65. Direction Sense
A man walks 20 m North, then 10 m West, then 20 m South.

**Explanation:**  
North & South cancel  
Net movement = 10 m West

### Q67. Syllogism
Statements:  
All cats are animals.  
All animals are living beings.

Conclusion:  
All cats are living beings.

**Answer:** ✅ True  

**Explanation:**  
Cats ⊂ Animals ⊂ Living beings

### Q69. Clock Reasoning
What is the angle between clock hands at 6:00?

**Answer:** 180°  

**Explanation:**  
Hour hand at 6 = 180°  
Minute hand at 12 = 0°  
Angle = **180°**

### Q70. Seating Arrangement
Four people A, B, C, D sit in a row.  
A is not at the end.  
B is to the left of A.  
C is to the right of D.

Who sits at the right end?

**Answer:** C  

**Explanation:**  
Only arrangement possible: B A D C  
Right end = **C**
