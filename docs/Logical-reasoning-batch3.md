### Q41. Number Series
**Find the next number:**  
2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

**Options:**  
A) 36  
B) 40  
C) 42  
D) 44  

**Answer:** C) 42  

**Explanation:**  
Pattern = n × (n+1)  
1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30  
Next = 6×7 = **42**

### Q54. Direction
