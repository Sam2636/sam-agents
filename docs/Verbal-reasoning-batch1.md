# TCS NQT Verbal Reasoning - Batch 1

## 1️⃣ Synonyms (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** Choose the word closest in meaning to **“Obsolete”**  
a) Modern  
b) Outdated  
c) Useful  
d) Popular  
**Answer:** b) Outdated  

**Q2:** Synonym for **“Ambiguous”**  
a) Clear  
b) Confusing  
c) Direct  
d) Simple  
**Answer:** b) Confusing  

**Q3:** Synonym of **“Candid”**  
a) Honest  
b) Evasive  
c) Secretive  
d) Quiet  
**Answer:** a) Honest  

**Q4:** Synonym of **“Eminent”**  
a) Unknown  
b) Famous  
c) Insignificant  
d) Ordinary  
**Answer:** b) Famous  

**Q5:** Synonym of **“Diligent”**  
a) Lazy  
b) Hardworking  
c) Careless  
d) Relaxed  
**Answer:** b) Hardworking

## 3️⃣ Sentence Correction (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** Choose the correct sentence:  
a) He don’t like ice cream.  
b) He doesn’t likes ice cream.  
c) He doesn’t like ice cream.  
d) He do not likes ice cream.  
**Answer:** c) He doesn’t like ice cream.  

**Q12:** Correct the sentence: *"She are going to the market tomorrow."*  
**Answer:** She **is** going to the market tomorrow.  

**Q13:** Correct the sentence: *"They has finished their work."*  
**Answer:** They **have** finished their work.  

**Q14:** Correct the sentence: *"I am agree with you."*  
**Answer:** I **agree** with you.  

**Q15:** Correct the sentence: *"He did not went to school yesterday."*  
**Answer:** He did not **go** to school yesterday.

## 4️⃣ Para Jumbles (Q16–Q18)

**Q16:** Arrange logically:  
A. Rahul walked into the library.  
B. The library was quiet and well-lit.  
C. He picked up a notebook from the shelf.  
D. He started reading the first chapter.  
**Answer:** A → B → C → D  

**Q17:** Arrange:  
A. She entered the hall and looked around.  
B. She took a seat near the window.  
C. The meeting was about to start.  
D. The chairman welcomed everyone.  
**Answer:** A → B → C → D  

**Q18:** Arrange:  
A. The sun rose over the mountains.  
B. Birds began to chirp.  
C. The village woke up slowly.  
D. Farmers prepared for their day.  
**Answer:** A → B → C → D

## 5️⃣ Reading Comprehension (Q19–Q23)

**Passage 1:**  
*"Global warming is causing glaciers to melt at an alarming rate. This leads to rising sea levels and unpredictable weather patterns."*  

**Q19:** What is the main effect of global warming according to the passage?  
a) Decrease in rainfall  
b) Rising sea levels  
c) Increase in forest cover  
d) Decrease in temperature  
**Answer:** b) Rising sea levels  

**Q20:** Which is NOT mentioned as an effect?  
a) Melting glaciers  
b) Rising sea levels  
c) Predictable weather  
d) Unpredictable weather  
**Answer:** c) Predictable weather  

**Passage 2:**  
*"Regular exercise improves mental health, reduces stress, and strengthens the immune system."*  

**Q21:** Which is NOT a benefit mentioned?  
a) Reduced stress  
b) Better mental health  
c) Stronger immunity  
d) Increased wealth  
**Answer:** d) Increased wealth  

**Q22:** What is a direct benefit of regular exercise?  
**Answer:** Improves mental health and reduces stress.  

**Q23:** What is strengthened by exercise according to the passage?  
**Answer:** The immune system
