# 50 Real-Life Scenario Questions for Pattern Identification

## Two Pointers

1. You’re standing at opposite ends of a playground with a friend and want to meet in the middle as fast as possible. Which strategy do you use?
2. Two people are holding ropes from opposite ends and want to find where they cross.
3. You have a sorted list of books by height and want to find two books that sum up to exactly 20 cm.
4. Two runners start at opposite ends of a track and try to catch each other.
5. You are matching pairs of socks from a sorted pile to minimize mismatch.

## Sliding Window

6. You are monitoring temperature for the last 7 days and want to find the maximum average temperature.
7. You want to find the longest period where a child’s heartbeat stays within a safe range.
8. You are tracking steps over a week and want to find the period with maximum activity.
9. You are watching traffic and want to find the maximum number of cars passing in any 10-minute interval.
10. You are streaming video and need to find the longest segment without buffering.

## Fast & Slow Pointers

11. You have a carousel spinning and want to detect if a horse loops back to the same position.
12. You want to find the middle step in a staircase to install a railing.
13. You are following a repeated dance pattern and want to detect if it repeats infinitely.
14. Two trains start at different speeds on the same track and may meet in a loop — how to detect a collision?
15. You are checking if a car is driving in circles in a parking lot.

## DFS / Backtracking

16. You are trying all possible routes in a maze to find all exits.
17. You are arranging seating combinations for a wedding table.
18. You are trying all PIN combinations for a lock.
19. You are exploring all possible moves in a chess game.
20. You are planning all possible itineraries for a holiday trip.
21. You are filling a Sudoku board recursively.
22. You want to generate all possible passwords using certain letters.
23. You are finding all possible unique pizza toppings combinations.
24. You are arranging books in all possible orders on a shelf.
25. You are exploring all possible paths to deliver packages in a city.

## BFS / Queue

26. You are spreading a rumor in a classroom and want to know how many people hear it each minute.
27. You are rescuing people in a building level by level.
28. You want to find the shortest path from your home to the supermarket through streets.
29. You are modeling water spreading from a leak in a house.
30. You are passing a message through a chain of friends and want to see who hears it first.
31. You are finding the shortest sequence of moves to solve a puzzle.
32. You are mapping the quickest route for delivery drones to reach customers.
33. You are checking infection spread in a hospital floor.
34. You are simulating email propagation in a company.
35. You are finding the minimum steps to reach a target in a game.

## Stack

36. You are stacking boxes and want to remove the top box first.
37. You are checking if nested boxes are correctly placed inside each other.
38. You are undoing actions in a text editor.
39. You are processing tasks in reverse order of arrival.
40. You are evaluating math expressions written in reverse polish notation.
41. You are balancing parentheses in a document.
42. You are navigating a website with “back” functionality.
43. You are reversing a list of commands.
44. You are checking the most recent operation in a calculator.
45. You are managing browser tabs in reverse order of usage.

## Queue

46. You are lining up people to buy tickets and serve them first-come-first-serve.
47. You are modeling customer service calls and answering in order of arrival.
48. You are managing a printer queue for documents.
49. You are scheduling tasks to run one after another in a workflow.
50. You are processing messages in a chat app in the order they arrive.

## How to Use These 50 Scenarios

1. Read the scenario carefully.
2. Ask yourself:

   * Is it about contiguous sequence? → Sliding Window
   * Is it about matching or reversal? → Stack
   * Is it about first-come-first-serve? → Queue
   * Is it about exploring paths/possibilities? → DFS / Backtracking
   * Is it about shortest distance or level-by-level exploration? → BFS
   * Is it about detecting loops or finding middle? → Fast & Slow Pointers
   * Is it about finding a pair or comparing two ends? → Two Pointers
3. Try to explain why you chose a pattern — that builds intuition.
