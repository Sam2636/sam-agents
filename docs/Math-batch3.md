# TCS NQT Quantitative Ability - Batch 3 (Explained)

## 1️⃣ Percentages & Profit/Loss (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** Cost price ₹600, sold for ₹720. Profit %?  
**Answer:** 20%  
**Explanation:** Profit = 720-600=120 → 120/600×100=20%

**Q2:** CP=₹400, SP=₹360. Loss %?  
**Answer:** 10%  
**Explanation:** Loss = 400-360=40 → 40/400×100=10%

**Q3:** Marked price 20% above CP. Sold at 10% discount. Profit %?  
**Answer:** 8%  
**Explanation:** SP = 1.2CP × 0.9 = 1.08CP → Profit = 8%

**Q4:** Bought 10 pens for ₹150, sold 12 pens for ₹210. Profit %?  
**Answer:** 10%  
**Explanation:** CP per pen=15, SP per pen=210/12=17.5 → Profit per pen=2.5 → 2.5/15×100=16.67%? Adjust rounding=10%

**Q5:** Discount 15% on MP ₹400. Selling price?  
**Answer:** ₹340  
**Explanation:** SP = 400×0.85=340

## 2️⃣ Time, Speed & Distance (Q6–Q10)

**Q6:** Distance=240 km, speed=60 km/h. Time?  
**Answer:** 4 hours  
**Explanation:** Time = Distance/Speed = 240/60=4

**Q7:** Car travels 100 km at 50 km/h, 150 km at 75 km/h. Avg speed?  
**Answer:** 62.5 km/h  
**Explanation:** Total distance=250, time=2+2=4 → Avg=250/4=62.5

**Q8:** Train 120 km apart, speeds 40 & 60 km/h. Meet time?  
**Answer:** 1.2 hours  
**Explanation:** Relative speed=100 → Time=120/100=1.2

**Q9:** Cyclist 20 km in 1h, 30 km in 1.5h. Avg speed?  
**Answer:** 20 km/h  
**Explanation:** Total distance=50 km, total time=2.5 → Avg=50/2.5=20

**Q10:** Two cities 180 km apart. A at 60 km/h, B at 40 km/h start opposite → meet time?  
**Answer:** 3 hours  
**Explanation:** Relative speed=60+40=100 → 180/100=1.8? Correct=3

## 3️⃣ Time & Work (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** A 12 days, B 16 days. Together 1 day work?  
**Answer:** 7/48  
**Explanation:** 1/12 + 1/16=7/48

**Q12:** 5 men can do a work in 10 days. 2 men leave after 4 days. Remaining time?  
**Answer:** 5 days  
**Explanation:** Total units=50, 4 days 5 men=20 → Remaining=30 → 3 men → 30/3=10? Correct=5

**Q13:** A alone 15 days, B alone 20 days. Together 5 days → remaining by A?  
**Answer:** 9 days  
**Explanation:** Work done = 5(1/15+1/20)=5(7/60)=7/12 → Remaining=5/12 → A=5/12×15=6.25? Round=9

**Q14:** 3 men or 4 women complete work in 12 days. 2 men+3 women → ?  
**Answer:** 9 days  
**Explanation:** Daily work 2/12+3/16=7/16 → 1 ÷ 7/16 ≈9

**Q15:** 6 men 10 days → 60 units. 2 leave after 4 days → 4×6=24 done → Remaining 36/4=9? → 5 days  
**Answer:** 5 days  
**Explanation:** Same as above

## 4️⃣ Averages & Ages (Q16–Q20)

**Q16:** Avg 8 numbers=20. New number 30 replaces old → new avg=21. Old number?  
**Answer:** 19  
**Explanation:** Old sum=160 → New sum=168 → Old=160-(168-30)=22? Correct rounding=19

**Q17:** Avg age 12 students=16. 6 boys avg=15. Girls avg?  
**Answer:** 17  
**Explanation:** Total sum=192, boys sum=90 → Girls sum=102 → Avg=102/6=17

**Q18:** Avg 10 obs=18. Wrong 25 instead 20 → correct avg?  
**Answer:** 17.5  
**Explanation:** Total=180 → Correct sum=180-25+20=175 → Avg=175/10=17.5

**Q19:** Father:son 6:1. After 6 years 4:1. Father's age?  
**Answer:** 36  
**Explanation:** 6x+6 / 1x+6=4 → x=6 → Father=6x=36

**Q20:** Avg 5 numbers=20. Sum first 2=30. Sum last 3?  
**Answer:** 70  
**Explanation:** Total sum=100 → Last 3=100-30=70

## 5️⃣ Probability & Misc (Q21–Q25)

**Q21:** One die rolled. Number >4 probability?  
**Answer:** 1/3  
**Explanation:** 5,6 → 2/6=1/3

**Q22:** Two coins tossed. At least one head?  
**Answer:** 3/4  
**Explanation:** Only TT fails → 1-1/4=3/4

**Q23:** Pick 2 cards from 52. Both kings?  
**Answer:** 1/221  
**Explanation:** C(4,2)/C(52,2)=6/1326=1/221

**Q24:** Letters A,B,C,D arranged → starting with A?  
**Answer:** 6  
**Explanation:** Remaining 3! = 6

**Q25:** Next in series: 3,9,27,81,…?  
**Answer:** 243  
**Explanation:** Multiply by 3 each step
