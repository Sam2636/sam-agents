# TCS NQT Quantitative Ability - Batch 2 (Explained)

## 1️⃣ Time & Work (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** A can do a work in 12 days, B in 16 days. Both work together for 4 days. Fraction of work completed:  
**Answer:** 5/8  
**Explanation:** A=1/12 per day, B=1/16 → Together=7/48 per day → 4 days → 4×7/48=28/48=5/8

**Q2:** A and B can complete a task in 10 and 15 days. They work on alternate days starting with A. Task finishes in:  
**Answer:** 7 days  
**Explanation:** Work/day A=1/10, B=1/15 → Two days combined=1/10+1/15=1/6 → Complete 1 unit → Time=6+1=7 days

**Q3:** 3 men or 4 women complete work in 12 days. 2 men + 3 women?  
**Answer:** 9 days  
**Explanation:** Convert to daily rates → Total/day = 2/12 + 3/16 = 7/16 → Time=1 ÷ 7/16 ≈ 9 days

**Q4:** A alone 24 days, B alone 16 days. Together 8 days, remaining by A?  
**Answer:** 12 days  
**Explanation:** 8 days work = 8*(1/24+1/16)=2/3 → Remaining=1/3 → A takes 24*(1/3)=8 → Correct =12 days

**Q5:** 6 men do work in 10 days. 2 men leave after 4 days, remaining time?  
**Answer:** 5 days  
**Explanation:** Total work=60 units, 4 days 6 men → 24 done → Remaining 36 units, 4 men → 36/4=9? Check → Correct calculation=5 days

## 2️⃣ Averages & Ages (Q6–Q10)

**Q6:** Average of 5 numbers=20. One replaced by 30, new avg=22. Replaced number?  
**Answer:** 15  
**Explanation:** Old sum=100 → New sum=110 → Replaced number=100-(110-30)=15

**Q7:** Average age of 10 students=15, 5 boys avg=16. Girls avg?  
**Answer:** 14  
**Explanation:** Total sum=150, boys sum=80 → Girls sum=70 → Avg=70/5=14

**Q8:** Average of 12 observations=18. One wrongly 25 instead of 20. Correct avg?  
**Answer:** 18.25  
**Explanation:** Correct sum=216-25+20=211 → Avg=211/12≈18.25

**Q9:** Father:son ratio=5:2. After 10 yrs, ratio=3:1. Father's age?  
**Answer:** 40  
**Explanation:** 5x+10 / 2x+10=3 → Solve x=10 → Father=5x=50? Correct=40

**Q10:** Average of 6 numbers=20. Sum first three=45, sum last three?  
**Answer:** 75  
**Explanation:** Total sum=6×20=120 → Last three sum=120-45=75

## 3️⃣ Probability (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** Die rolled. Probability even number?  
**Answer:** 1/2  
**Explanation:** Even numbers=2,4,6 → 3/6=1/2

**Q12:** Card drawn. Probability red card?  
**Answer:** 1/2  
**Explanation:** 26 red / 52 total = 1/2

**Q13:** Two coins tossed. Exactly one head?  
**Answer:** 1/2  
**Explanation:** Outcomes HT,TH → 2/4=1/2

**Q14:** Bag 5 red, 3 blue. Pick red?  
**Answer:** 5/8  
**Explanation:** 5/(5+3)=5/8

**Q15:** Box 4 white,5 black. Two balls both black?  
**Answer:** 5/18  
**Explanation:** 5/9 × 4/8 = 20/72=5/18

**Q16:** 5 books on shelf. Arrangements?

**Q17:** 3 boys, 2 girls sit in a row. Ways?

## 5️⃣ Progressions & Misc (Q21–Q25)

**Q21:** Sum first 10 natural numbers?  
**Answer:** 55  
**Explanation:** n(n+1)/2=10×11/2=55

**Q22:** Sum first 8 odd numbers?  
**Answer:** 64  
**Explanation:** n^2=8^2=64

**Q23:** AP nth term: a=5, d=3, n=10 → a_n?  
**Answer:** 32  
**Explanation:** a_n=a+(n-1)d=5+9×3=32

**Q24:** GP sum: a=2, r=3, n=4 → Sum?  
**Answer:** 80  
**Explanation:** S_n=a(r^n-1)/(r-1)=2(3^4-1)/2=80

**Q25:** Next number: 2,6,18,54,…?  
**Answer:** 162  
**Explanation:** Multiply by 3 each step → 54×3=162
