# TCS NQT Verbal Reasoning - Batch 3

## 1️⃣ Cloze Test (Q1–Q5)

**Q1:** Fill in the blank:  
*"She was very ______ about the results of the exam."*  
a) anxious  
b) calm  
c) careless  
d) lazy  
**Answer:** a) anxious  

**Q2:** Fill in the blank:  
*"The manager asked him to ______ the report by Monday."*  
a) submit  
b) avoid  
c) destroy  
d) ignore  
**Answer:** a) submit  

**Q3:** Fill in the blank:  
*"He ______ his keys while leaving the house."*  
a) forgot  
b) remembers  
c) finds  
d) keeps  
**Answer:** a) forgot  

**Q4:** Fill in the blank:  
*"The government is planning to ______ new policies for education."*  
a) implement  
b) ignore  
c) avoid  
d) destroy  
**Answer:** a) implement  

**Q5:** Fill in the blank:  
*"She was praised for her ______ efforts in the project."*  
a) diligent  
b) careless  
c) lazy  
d) indifferent  
**Answer:** a) diligent

## 2️⃣ Error Spotting (Q6–Q10)

**Q6:** Identify the error: *"He don’t have enough time to finish the work."*  
**Answer:** "don’t" → Correct: "doesn’t"  

**Q7:** Identify the error: *"Everyone are invited to the party."*  
**Answer:** "are" → Correct: "is"  

**Q8:** Identify the error: *"She can sings beautifully."*  
**Answer:** "sings" → Correct: "sing"  

**Q9:** Identify the error: *"I have went to the market yesterday."*  
**Answer:** "went" → Correct: "gone"  

**Q10:** Identify the error: *"Neither of the options are suitable."*  
**Answer:** "are" → Correct: "is"

## 3️⃣ Sentence Completion (Q11–Q15)

**Q11:** Complete the sentence:  
*"If it rains tomorrow, we ______ the trip."*  
a) will cancel  
b) cancel  
c) cancelled  
d) cancels  
**Answer:** a) will cancel  

**Q12:** Complete:  
*"The book, as well as the notebooks, ______ on the table."*  
a) is  
b) are  
c) was  
d) were  
**Answer:** a) is  

**Q13:** Complete:  
*"Neither John nor his friends ______ interested in the event."*  
a) is  
b) are  
c) was  
d) were  
**Answer:** b) are  

**Q14:** Complete:  
*"Had I known about the traffic, I ______ earlier."*  
a) would leave  
b) will leave  
c) would have left  
d) leave

## 4️⃣ Critical Reasoning (Q16–Q18)

**Q16:** Read and answer:  
*"All dogs are animals. Some animals are cats. Can we conclude that some dogs are cats?"*  
**Answer:** No  

**Q17:** Read and answer:  
*"Every student who studies hard scores well. Ravi scores well. Can we conclude Ravi studies hard?"*  
**Answer:** Not necessarily  

**Q18:** Read and answer:  
*"If all managers are supervisors and all supervisors are employees, are all managers employees?"*  
**Answer:** Yes

## 5️⃣ Advanced Reading Comprehension (Q19–Q23)

**Passage 1:**  
*"Climate change is affecting agriculture worldwide. Crop yields are decreasing due to erratic weather patterns, pests, and diseases."*  

**Q19:** Main effect of climate change:  
**Answer:** Decrease in crop yields  

**Q20:** Cause of decreased crop yields:  
**Answer:** Erratic weather, pests, diseases  

**Passage 2:**  
*"The rapid growth of e-commerce has transformed consumer behavior. Online shopping is convenient but poses challenges for traditional retailers."*  

**Q21:** Benefit of e-commerce:  
**Answer:** Convenience for consumers  

**Q22:** Challenge posed:  
**Answer:** Traditional retailers face difficulties  

**Q23:** Implied statement:  
**Answer:** E-commerce is changing market dynamics
