    🧠 TCS NQT – Logical Reasoning¶

    📦 Batch 5 (Questions 81–100)¶

    Q81. Number Series¶

    Find the next number:
    7, 14, 28, 56, ?

    Answer: 112

    Explanation:
    Each number × 2
    56 × 2 = 112

    Q82. Alphabet Series¶

    Find the missing letter:
    C, F, J, O, ?

    Answer: U

    Explanation:
    Alphabet positions:
    C(3), F(6), J(10), O(15)
    Differences: +3, +4, +5
    Next difference = +6
    15 + 6 = 21 → U

    Q83. Coding–Decoding¶

    If TABLE = 20-1-2-12-5, then CHAIR = ?

    Answer: 3-8-1-9-18

    Explanation:
    Alphabet positions:
    C=3, H=8, A=1, I=9, R=18

    Q84. Blood Relation¶

    Pointing to a girl, a man said,
    “She is the daughter of my wife’s brother.”

    Who is the girl to the man?

    Answer: Niece

    Explanation:
    Wife’s brother = Brother-in-law
    His daughter = Niece

    Q85. Direction Sense¶

    A person walks 5 m South, then 5 m East, then 5 m North.
    How far is he from the starting point?

    Answer: 5 meters East

    Explanation:
    South and North cancel
    Net movement = 5 m East

    Q86. Odd One Out¶

    A) Rose
    B) Lotus
    C) Lily
    D) Grass

    Answer: D) Grass

    Explanation:
    Grass is not a flower

    Q87. Syllogism¶

    Statements:
    All engineers are graduates.
    Some graduates are teachers.

    Conclusion:
    Some engineers are teachers.

    Answer: ❌ Cannot be determined

    Explanation:
    No direct relation between engineers and teachers

    Q88. Number Analogy¶

    5 : 125 :: 4 : ?

    Answer: 64

    Explanation:
    5³ = 125
    4³ = 64

    Q89. Clock Reasoning¶

    What is the angle between the hands at 9:00?

    Answer: 90°

    Explanation:
    Hour hand at 9 = 270°
    Minute hand at 12 = 0°
    Smaller angle = 90°

    Q90. Seating Arrangement¶

    Six people sit in a row.
    A is left of B.
    C is right of D.
    E is at the extreme left.
    F is at the extreme right.

    Who sits in the middle?

    Answer: B or C

    Explanation:
    Multiple valid arrangements → middle not uniquely fixed

    Q91. Alphabet Coding¶

    If A=2, B=4, C=6, …
    Find value of DOG

    Answer: 52

    Explanation:
    D=8, O=30, G=14
    8 + 30 + 14 = 52

    Q92. Mirror Image¶

    Which alphabet looks the same in mirror (vertical)?

    Answer: M

    Explanation:
    M is vertically symmetrical

    Q93. Mathematical Reasoning¶

    If 4x + 6 = 22, find x.

    Answer: 4

    Explanation:
    4x = 16
    x = 4

    Q94. Venn Diagram¶

    All doctors are educated.
    Some educated people are rich.

    Which conclusion is correct?

    Answer: Some doctors may be rich

    Explanation:
    Doctors ⊂ Educated
    Rich overlaps partially

    Q95. Odd Pair¶

    A) Keyboard – Type
    B) Knife – Cut
    C) Pen – Erase
    D) Spoon – Eat

    Answer: C) Pen – Erase

    Explanation:
    Pen is not used to erase

    Q96. Pattern¶

    2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ?

    Answer: 21

    Explanation:
    Each number = sum of previous two

    Q97. Direction¶

    Facing West, turn left, then right, then left.
    Which direction now?

    Answer: South

    Explanation:
    West → Left = South
    South → Right = West
    West → Left = South

    Q98. Alphabet Order¶

    How many letters are between E and P?

    Answer: 10

    Explanation:
    E(5), P(16)
    16 − 5 − 1 = 10

    Q99. Statement–Conclusion¶

    Statement: All mobiles are phones.

    Conclusion:
    Some phones are mobiles.

    Answer: ✅ True

    Explanation:
    Mobiles ⊂ Phones
    So some phones are mobiles

    Q100. Number Pattern¶

    1, 8, 27, 64, ?

    Answer: 125

    Explanation:
    Perfect cubes:
    1³, 2³, 3³, 4³
    Next = 5³ = 125
