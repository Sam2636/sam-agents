    🧠 Logical Reasoning — Batch 2 (40 / 100)¶

    TCS NQT Exam Style — With Step-by-Step Explanations

    🔢 1. Number Series¶

    Q21¶

    5, 11, 23, 47, ?

    Explanation: - 5 × 2 + 1 = 11 - 11 × 2 + 1 = 23 - 23 × 2 + 1 = 47

    Next: - 47 × 2 + 1 = 95

    Answer: 95

    Q22¶

    1, 1, 2, 6, 24, ?

    Explanation: - Factorial pattern: - 1! = 1 - 2! = 2 - 3! = 6 - 4! = 24

    Next = 5! = 120

    Answer: 120

    Q23¶

    2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?

    Explanation: - Pattern: n² + 1 - 1²+1 = 2 - 2²+1 = 5 - 3²+1 = 10 - 4²+1 = 17 - 5²+1 = 26

    Next = 6² + 1 = 37

    Answer: 37

    Q24¶

    100, 81, 64, 49, ?

    Explanation: - Squares in descending order: - 10², 9², 8², 7²

    Next = 6² = 36

    Answer: 36

    Q25¶

    7, 14, 28, 56, ?

    Explanation: - Each term × 2

    56 × 2 = 112

    Answer: 112

    🔐 2. Coding–Decoding¶

    Q26¶

    If FLOWER = GMPXFS, then GARDEN = ?

    Explanation: - Each letter is shifted +1

    G → H
    A → B
    R → S
    D → E
    E → F
    N → O

    Answer: HBSEFO

    Q27¶

    If A = 1, Z = 26, find the value of DOG

    Explanation: - D = 4 - O = 15 - G = 7

    Sum = 4 + 15 + 7 = 26

    Answer: 26

    Q28¶

    If BAT = 21, CAT = 24, then DOG = ?

    Explanation: - B(2) + A(1) + T(20) = 23 ❌
    (Given 21 → pattern is sum − 2)

    Check CAT: - C(3)+A(1)+T(20)=24 ✔

    Apply to DOG: - D(4)+O(15)+G(7)=26

    Answer: 26

    Q29¶

    If CLOCK = DMPDL, then WATCH = ?

    Explanation: - Each letter +1

    W → X
    A → B
    T → U
    C → D
    H → I

    Answer: XBUDI

    🧩 3. Logical Puzzles¶

    Q30¶

    Some pens are pencils.
    All pencils are erasers.
    Conclusion: Some pens are erasers.

    Explanation: - Pens ∩ Pencils ≠ ∅ - Pencils ⊂ Erasers

    Answer: True

    Q31¶

    All roses are flowers.
    Some flowers are red.
    Conclusion: Some roses are red.

    Explanation: - No guaranteed overlap

    Answer: Cannot be determined

    Q32¶

    Which number does not belong?
    2, 3, 5, 9, 11

    Explanation: - All are prime except 9

    Answer: 9

    Q33¶

    Find the odd one out:
    Circle, Square, Triangle, Cube

    Explanation: - Cube is 3D, others are 2D

    Answer: Cube

    👨‍👩‍👦 4. Blood Relations¶

    Q34¶

    Pointing to a man, a woman says,
    "He is my mother’s brother’s son."

    Explanation: - Mother’s brother = Uncle - Uncle’s son = Cousin

    Answer: Cousin

    Q35¶

    A is the father of B.
    B is the sister of C.
    How is A related to C?

    Explanation: - A is father of B - B and C are siblings

    Answer: Father

    🧭 5. Direction Sense¶

    Q36¶

    A person walks 15 m north, then 15 m west, then 15 m south.
    Distance from starting point?

    Explanation: - North and south cancel - Remaining = 15 m west

    Answer: 15 m

    Q37¶

    Facing south, turn right, then left, then left.
    Final direction?

    Explanation: - South → Right → West - West → Left → South - South → Left → East

    Answer: East

    🧠 6. Clocks & Calendars¶

    Q38¶

    How many times do clock hands overlap in 24 hours?

    Explanation: - 11 times in 12 hours - 22 times in 24 hours

    Answer: 22

    Q39¶

    If today is Friday, what day will it be after 20 days?

    Explanation: - 20 mod 7 = 6 - Friday + 6 = Thursday

    Answer: Thursday

    🧩 7. Miscellaneous Logic¶

    Q40¶

    Which word cannot be formed using letters of EDUCATION?

    A) Date
    B) Action
    C) Unit
    D) Auction

    Explanation: - All letters available except U is only once
    - "Unit" needs two U’s

    Answer: Unit
